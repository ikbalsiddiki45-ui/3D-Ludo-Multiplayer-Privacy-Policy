How to Play Ludo

        
      

      

        
1. 🎲 Roll a 6 to bring a token out from home yard.

        
2. 🏁 Move tokens clockwise around the 3D board.

        
3. ⚔️ Land on an opponent's token to capture it and send it home!

        
4. 🎁 You roll again after a 6, after you capture an opponent token, and when one of your tokens reaches the middle home.

        
5. 👑 Be the first player to get all 4 tokens home to WIN!
