1. What the App does

3D Ludo Multiplayer is a 3D Ludo board game. You can play against the computer, pass-and-play on one phone, or play online with friends in private rooms. The App includes accounts and login, virtual coins, a shop and inventory, a ranking list, a friend list and chat.

2. Information we collect

a) Account information

  • Email sign-up: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot read your password.
  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and a unique Google account identifier, provided by Google.
  • Facebook login: your public profile (name) and a unique Facebook identifier, provided by Meta. We do not request your email, friends list or posts from Facebook.
  • Guest: an anonymous random user ID. No email is needed.
  • A unique user ID (UID) created by Firebase, and the display name you choose.

b) Game and profile data

  • Display name, avatar (emoji), friend code, online/offline status.
  • Game statistics: matches played, wins and ranking score.
  • Virtual coins, items you bought or equipped (avatars, dice, boards, pawns) and the date of your last daily bonus.

c) Social and multiplayer data

  • Your friend list, friend requests and room invitations.
  • Chat messages you send in a room or privately to a friend, with your display name and a timestamp.
  • Room information (room name, code, players) and the game moves needed to keep all players’ boards in sync.

d) Device, advertising and diagnostic data

  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, app interactions and approximate location derived from IP, as described in Google’s policies.
  • Firebase services may process technical information such as IP address and connection data to operate the service.

e) What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs or payment information. Coins are virtual and cannot be bought with real money in this version of the App.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and log you in.
  • To run online rooms, matches, chat, friends, coins, shop and ranking.
  • To show your name, avatar and score to other players (for example in the ranking list, a friend’s list or a room).
  • To show ads and to measure ad performance (Google AdMob).
  • To fix bugs, prevent abuse and improve the App.
  • To answer support requests sent to our email.

4. What other players can see

Your display name, avatar, ranking score, wins and games played are visible to other logged-in players (for example in the Ranking list). Your friend code can be used by others to send you a friend request. Your email address is never shown to other players. Messages you send are visible to the people in that room or to the friend you are chatting with. Do not share personal or sensitive information in chat.

5. Third-party services

The App uses the following services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In – policies.google.com/privacy
  • Facebook Login (Meta) – facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Google AdMob – policies.google.com/technologies/ads

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with these service providers so the App can work, and where required by law.

6. Advertising

The App shows banner and interstitial (full-screen) ads from Google AdMob. Ads are not shown during a match. Ad personalization depends on your Android settings; you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads. During development and testing the App may show Google test ads.

7. Where data is stored and how long

Account and game data is stored in Google Firebase. Our Realtime Database is located in Singapore (asia-southeast1); Google may process data in other countries where it operates. We keep your account data until you delete your account. Room data, room chat messages and game moves are deleted when the room is closed (which happens as soon as any player leaves). Private chat messages are deleted when either friend removes the other or when an account is deleted. Room invitations expire after 30 minutes.

8. Delete your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the App: Home → Profile → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, coins, items, ranking, friends, friend requests and private chats. Full instructions are on our Account Deletion page. You can also email ikbalsiddiki45@gmail.com to request deletion.

9. Your rights and choices

  • You can change your display name and avatar in the App.
  • You can remove friends, leave rooms and log out whenever you like.
  • You can ask us (by email) for a copy of, a correction to, or deletion of your personal data. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use Firebase security rules, authentication and encrypted connections (HTTPS) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
